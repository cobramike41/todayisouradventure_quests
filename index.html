📜 Quest Instructions

  • The Search: Find Mike and Amy together! We’ll be at the social hours, seminars, meals, or hanging out by the fire or on the patio of our Brinkley Z 3400.
  • The Selfie: Grab a quick selfie with both of us.
  • The Submission: Message your selfie and your Campsite Number to us via Facebook Messenger.
    • 💡 Hint: You can scan the Yellow "?" on Mike or Amy’s cup to instantly open the link and turn in your quest!
    • The campsite number is required so we can deliver your loot!
  • The Deadline: All entries must be messaged by 4:00 PM on the last full day of the rally.

⚖️ The Rules

  • The Duo: To count, the selfie must include both Mike and Amy.
  • Respect the Rig: We love visitors, but please be mindful of quiet hours and private downtime at the campsites.
